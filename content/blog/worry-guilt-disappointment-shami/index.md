These are the phrases I used in [Guilt, Maturity, and Loving Well](/blog/guilt-maturity-and-loving-well/) to write about the holidays, my family, and the guilt I used to carry. I'm a learner writing down what I actually use, not a teacher. Treat this as my notes. The short examples under each phrase are my own practice sentences and haven't been checked by a native speaker yet.

Here's the sentence from the diary that holds most of them:

{{< example ar="جزء كبير من السبب اللي كان يخليني أشيل هم الأعياد هو إني كنت أحس بالذنب، وكأني عم بخيّب أمل أهلي." en="A big part of what made me dread the holidays was that I felt guilty, as if I were letting my family down." >}}

## Worry: أشيل هم

{{< word ar="أشيل هَمّ" sh_ipa="ʔaˈʃiːl hamm" meaning="to worry about; to dread (lit. to carry the worry of)" root="ش · ي · ل" pos="phrase" note="In the diary: «اللي كان يخليني أشيل هم الأعياد» — what made me dread the holidays. The thing you're worried about comes straight after هم." >}}

{{% examples title="Examples" titleAr="أمثلة" %}}
{{< example ar="لا تشيل هم، كل شي رح يزبط." en="Don't worry, it'll all work out." tag="masc." >}}
{{< example ar="لا تشيلي هم، كل شي رح يزبط." en="Don't worry, it'll all work out." tag="fem." >}}
{{< example ar="بشيل هم الامتحان من هلّق." en="I'm already dreading the exam." >}}
{{% /examples %}}

## Guilt: أحس بالذنب

{{< word ar="أحسّ بالذنب" meaning="to feel guilty" pos="phrase" note="In the diary: «كنت أحس بالذنب» — I used to feel guilty. حسّ بـ = to feel (something); ذنب = guilt, fault." >}}

{{% examples title="Examples" titleAr="أمثلة" %}}
{{< example ar="بحس بالذنب إني ما اتصلت فيها." en="I feel guilty that I didn't call her." >}}
{{< example ar="ليش حاسس بالذنب؟ إنت ما عملت إشي غلط." en="Why do you feel guilty? You didn't do anything wrong." tag="masc." >}}
{{% /examples %}}

## Disappointing someone: خيّب أمل

{{< word ar="بخيّب أمل" sh_ipa="bxaˈjjeb ʔamal" meaning="I disappoint (someone); from خيّب أمل, to let someone's hope down" root="خ · ي · ب" pos="verb phrase" note="In the diary: «كأني عم بخيّب أمل أهلي» — as if I were disappointing my family. Whoever is disappointed goes right after أمل: أمل أهلي، أملك، أملي." >}}

The same entry also uses the other direction. **خاب أمل** means someone's hope fell through, so _they_ are disappointed, with nobody doing it to them: «أخاف يخيب أمل الناس اللي بحبوني فيي» — I was afraid the people who love me would be disappointed. The short form you'll hear is **خاب أملي**, "I was disappointed."

{{% examples title="Examples" titleAr="أمثلة" %}}
{{< example ar="ما بدي أخيّب أملك." en="I don't want to disappoint you." >}}
{{< example ar="خيّبت أملي." en="You disappointed me." note="Written the same for masc. and fem. when unvowelled." >}}
{{< example ar="خاب أملي لما ما إجا." en="I was disappointed when he didn't come." >}}
{{% /examples %}}

## Being hard on yourself: بعذّب حالي

{{< word ar="بعذّب حالي" sh_ipa="baʕaˈddeb ħaːli" meaning="I torture / torment myself" root="ع · ذ · ب" pos="verb + reflexive" note="The diary's key line: «بس أغلب هاد كان أنا عم بعذّب حالي.» حال + a suffix works as '-self': حالي، حالك، حاله." >}}

{{% examples title="Examples" titleAr="أمثلة" %}}
{{< example ar="بطّل تعذّب حالك." en="Stop torturing yourself." tag="masc." >}}
{{< example ar="بطّلي تعذّبي حالك." en="Stop torturing yourself." tag="fem." >}}
{{% /examples %}}
